## Description

Oh noes! Your spaceship has gotten stranded on a planet with the FILTHY humans! Unfortunately on your voyage, your shipmate went out for a stroll on on this backwards planet causing you to delay your honeymoon plans.

No matter, biomass is biomass! It just might take a little longer to harvest is all, but that's about all this lifeform is good for anyway.

We may be able to extract some knowledge from some of the less mediocre apes, I've heard they wear lab coats.

Area 17 is a fun and fast paced shoot em' up and then abduct them for spare parts game. You must abduct humans in order to get enough biomass to upgrade your ship while trying to find your missing beloved stranded on the ground. Unfortunately, while you've narrowed their location down to a fairly small area, you're going to have to find them the old fashioned way.

Prime your lazers and shine yer probes! It's abduction time at Area 17!!! Yeehaw!

SpringCabal's second Ludum Dare entry!
