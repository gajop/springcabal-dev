Method and source data

Timings

At least five clean alternating pairs; 300 warmup + 600 measured steps; one draw per step; 1024×736; VSync/sound off. A failure or CPU/GPU overlap excludes both members. Rejected attempts remain visible.

World recordings

Fresh runs with a read-only observer, derived from Ship Game’s recording approach. All observed units and sampled projectiles are retained. They show workload and movement; serialization makes them unsuitable timing baselines.

CPU sampling

Fresh runs without the world observer. Linux perf samples user CPU at 499 Hz with DWARF stacks and JIT names. Sampling perturbs execution; stack proportions diagnose hot paths, rather than replace paired timings.

The engine and SDK remain pinned to release 2026.10.25. NVIDIA hardware rendering is required; the existing runner rejects llvmpipe and other software renderers. The cgroup guard retains global/local CPU pressure. The GPU guard requires ten quiet seconds and monitors competing GPU processes during each run. All engine threads are pinned to the eight P cores; E cores are excluded.
