Reproduce and audit

Method and source data

Timings

Five alternating pairs; 300 warmup steps, 600 measured steps, one draw per step, 1024×736, VSync off, sound off. Both members of a pair are excluded if either fails or crosses the CPU-pressure threshold. One Flove pair is excluded.

World recordings

Fresh runs with a read-only observer, derived from Ship Game’s recording approach. All observed units and sampled projectiles are retained. They show workload and movement; serialization makes them unsuitable timing baselines.

CPU sampling

Fresh runs without the world observer. Linux perf samples user CPU at 499 Hz with DWARF stacks and JIT names. Sampling perturbs execution; stack proportions diagnose hot paths, rather than replace paired timings.

The engine and SDK remain pinned to release 2026.10.25. NVIDIA hardware rendering is required; the existing runner rejects llvmpipe and other software renderers. The cgroup pressure guard retains both global and local readings and fails closed. It does not certify exclusive GPU access.

Native work and visible output are not identical across all ports. Flove retains Rust-only visual improvements; Hunted startup decisions can vary; TTLD uses shadows on in both selected controls; the 100-emitter fire row has unequal particle caps. The scene lab exposes populations and traces alongside the times.
